# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome different experience levels, discuss ideas rather than people, and give maintainers and contributors room to correct mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, spam, deceptive promotion, and publishing another person's private information.

## Scope

This standard applies to Issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, and other public spaces managed by this repository.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that do not follow these standards and may restrict participation when necessary. To report conduct concerns privately, contact the repository owner through the contact options on the [HackSing GitHub profile](https://github.com/HackSing).

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled with reasonable confidentiality. Enforcement decisions are made by the repository maintainers.
